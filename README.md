# Balatro-Porkify

The Porkify Mod adds a variety of new content to Balatro, while also slightly balancing the game.

## Additions
- 65 Jokers
- 10 Decks
- 2 Vouchers
- 48 Porkify Cards (Porkify Cards can significantly change the course of any run)
- 7 Enhancements
- 5 Editions
- 8 Booster Packs
- 15 Blinds
- 2 Stickers
- 6 Stakes
